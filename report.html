Plain-English glossary

Sharpe ratioReturn earned per unit of risk taken. Around 0.5 is decent, 1 is strong, above 2 on real trading is rare and deserves suspicion.
DrawdownHow far the account falls from its previous high before recovering. It tells you how bad the pain gets.
Unseen dataThe most recent part of history, kept aside and never used when choosing the strategy's settings. It is the fairest test available.
Basis point (bp)One hundredth of one percent. 2 bp of cost means paying 0.02% every time the position changes.
OverfittingTuning a strategy until it fits past noise. It looks great on history and then fails on new data.

What this report cannot tell you

This report tests simple rules on past prices. It cannot account for changing markets, liquidity, taxes, or how you would behave in a real drawdown. Results on a few years of data can be luck either way. A green verdict means "worth testing further", never "safe to trade". Not investment advice.
